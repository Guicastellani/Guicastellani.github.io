About Me

Guilherme Castellani Magri

Since I was a kid, I’ve been passionate about games — not just playing them, but being curious about how they worked, how they were built, and how design and technology came together to create entire worlds. That curiosity grew into a real passion for tech, creativity, and constant learning.

Today, I work as a web developer, graphic designer, and video editor, combining technical skills with visual storytelling to bring ideas to life. I love diving into new challenges, learning something new with every project, and evolving a little more each day.

This portfolio is a reflection of that journey — a space where I share some of the things I’ve built, designed, and edited along the way. Feel free to explore, and let’s connect if you’d like to collaborate!
